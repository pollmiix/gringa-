SHOPPING BAG
The shared shopping bag opens as a right-side drawer on catalogue and product
pages. Select a size, then use ADD TO BAG. Quantity, removal, totals, header
item counts and recommended products update immediately. Choices are saved
in this browser. Local file previews also carry bag selections between the
storefront pages; no customer contact details are stored in that navigation.
The drawer has fixed shipping/subtotal/checkout controls and a scrollable body.
Shipping is calculated at checkout by default. Set freteGratis to true in
loja-config.js only if your store actually offers free shipping.
The Check Out button does not process orders or payments. Until a checkout
service is connected, it shows an unavailable message and keeps the items.
Upload store-cart.js and store-cart.css with the other storefront files.

CHECKOUT
Check Out opens checkout.html with the current cart, sizes and quantities.
The responsive English page includes contact/delivery fields and order summary.
Express wallets are visual placeholders and disabled until connected. Discounts,
shipping and taxes are not calculated yet. Submit validates the contact/address
form and explains that payment is not connected; it never places an order.
Customer address/email data is not saved or sent anywhere. Marketing consent is
unchecked by default and is not submitted to a mailing list.
To enable payment later, connect a backend that creates payment sessions with
your provider. Revalidate item prices, quantities, shipping and taxes server-side;
use hosted payment fields and webhooks. Never put secret keys in loja-config.js.

Checkout footer: six expandable FAQ entries, customer service Chat Now (uses configured WhatsApp), current-year copyright and a privacy information dialog. Replace provisional FAQ answers and publish your full privacy policy before opening ordering.

Payment section: selectable card, Apple Pay, PayPal and Klarna placeholders; no provider connected and no raw card inputs. Different billing address fields enable and become required only when selected. Save-information preference does not store customer data. Pay now validates the form and confirms that no charge or order occurred.

Shipping display configured as requested: free worldwide DHL shipping, 1–3 Business Days. Set freteGratis and freteDescricao in loja-config.js to update this display. Carrier booking and shipping rate integration are not connected.
