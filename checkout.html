FAQ'S

WHEN WILL MY ORDER BE SHIPPED?

Shipping dates depend on the product and its availability. Pre-order shipping dates must be confirmed before payment. Contact customer service for an update on your order.

WHEN AND HOW CAN I USE MY
TRACKING NUMBER?

Once your order has shipped and tracking is available, use the carrier's tracking link to follow your delivery. Tracking updates may take time to appear after dispatch.

HOW DO I RETURN AN ORDER?

Contact customer service with your order number and the items you would like to return. Confirm return eligibility, instructions and any applicable costs before sending items back.

TYPE OF ORDERS (MIXED, PRE-ORDER,
IN STOCK)

In-stock orders contain items available for dispatch, subject to confirmation. Pre-orders contain items with a later shipping date. Mixed orders contain both types; delivery arrangements must be confirmed before payment.

HOW DO I GET MY AFFILIATE TRACKING LINK?

Affiliate registration and tracking links are not available yet. Contact customer service for information about future affiliate opportunities.
