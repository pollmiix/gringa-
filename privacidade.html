Privacy policy

Updated November 1, 2026.

This policy explains how Aloha, operated by Aloha All Bless Confeccoes LTDA, CNPJ 02.115.915/0001-78, handles personal data. Privacy requests can be sent to aloha82@gmail.com.

Information and purposes

  • Technical hosting requests, including IP addresses and request information, are used to operate and secure the website.
  • Shopping bag items, sizes, quantities and wishlist preferences are stored locally in your browser.
  • Selected products are sent to our server and Stripe to start checkout. Stripe collects the name, email, phone, shipping address and payment information required to process the purchase and delivery.
  • Information you provide by email or phone is used to answer your request and handle orders, returns and refunds.

Providers and sharing

We use data to fulfil purchases, provide support, secure our services and comply with legal obligations. Vercel hosts the website and Stripe processes payment. Necessary delivery information is shared with the carrier handling your order. We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

See the Stripe privacy policy and Vercel privacy policy. Our business is in Brazil and service providers may process information internationally under their policies and applicable legal requirements.

Cookies and local storage

Essential storage keeps your bag, wishlist and cookie preferences. Optional analytics and personalization remain off unless you consent. No optional analytics or personalization tools are installed in this version of the store.

Your consent preference is saved for 180 days in the same browser. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change it. You can remove bag and wishlist items in the interface or clear site data in your browser. Stripe-hosted pages have their own privacy settings.

Retention and security

Order and support records are retained as needed for the stated purposes and applicable legal obligations. The store uses HTTPS. Card fields are hosted by Stripe; full card details are not stored in this website's public code.

Your requests

You can request information, access, correction, deletion where applicable, or withdrawal of consent by email. We may need proportionate identity verification and may retain records required by law. Please do not send passwords or card security codes.
